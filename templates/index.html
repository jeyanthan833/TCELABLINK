HOW TCELABLINK WORKS

From physical equipment to a digital record

01

Register

Authorized staff maintain equipment records, location, model numbers, and technical specifications.

02

Identify

Printable QR tags on physical equipment allow lab technicians to instantly scan and access records.

03

Monitor

Staff monitor real-time availability, overdue alerts, and defect logs from the centralized staff dashboard.

04

Track movement

Students submit borrow requests and technicians digitally record equipment issue, handover, and return.

PROJECT DIRECTION

Digital management first. Controlled borrowing workflows.

Developed as a Design Thinking engineering prototype, TCELABLINK centralizes laboratory asset tracking, maintains inspection audit trails, and streamlines student equipment utilization.
